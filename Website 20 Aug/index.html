Come in. This is where I keep my work, my photography, and the things I write when I need to think them through.

I sell software for a living. These days I'm a Senior Account Executive at Coverflex, working with Portuguese companies on compensation, benefits and the changing shape of work. Most of the job is conversation: with founders, HR teams and people trying to make better decisions.

AI is now part of how I work every day. I use it to prepare better, move faster, and clear away much of the work around the conversation, so I can pay more attention when another person is actually in front of me. Technology can improve the work. It doesn't replace the human part of it.

Away from the laptop, I photograph, read theology and philosophy, and I'm a husband and father of five. That explains most of the noise in this house, and puts the rest of it in perspective.
